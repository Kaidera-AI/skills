# Fusion

Use Fusion when independent construction and synthesis can improve a difficult task enough to justify the extra work. A simpler single run can satisfy a task that does not benefit from the extra work. Decide within the customer's existing purpose and cost/call budget.

## Cast selection

Resolve architect, builder and any judge through the native catalogue before execution, with explicit nonempty provider-qualified identities. Preserve the customer's specified cast. A deliberately same-model pair requires an explicit choice and cannot be described as model diversity.

An unresolved builder requires refusal, not reuse of the current or architect model.

Native defaults or historical source fallbacks are not evidence of a valid chosen cast. If the installed contract cannot express or verify the requested cast, return the unsupported requirement instead of inventing a setting.

## Budget and call plan

State which extra work the proposed cast performs and account for the additional builder, judge and synthesis calls permitted by the native workflow. Include native availability/quota and the customer's budget. Unknown cost or authorization does not mean unlimited calls.

An extra call outside the approved purpose or budget requires refusal before dispatch.

## Effect and learning

Report the identities that actually built and combined the result, the evidence for agreement/disagreement, discarded alternatives and remaining uncertainty. Native learning or bandit adaptation is a separate capability: require its owner/effect receipt before claiming it works. A record of a proposed cast is not evidence of its execution or learning.
