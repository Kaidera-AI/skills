# Human file review record

Status: PENDING. Copy into the project record; an AI must not fill a human verdict.

- Candidate: frozen content commit/tree or artifact manifest digest.
- Plan/policy revisions; scope; full changed-file inventory (including deletions).
- AI reviewer identity and verdict; findings and their dispositions.
- Verification/QA evidence and limitations.
- Human reviewer: authenticated identity.
- Decision/date: APPROVE / REQUEST CHANGES / REJECT.
- Per-file findings or explicit coverage; rationale; conditions; residual risk.
- Separate integration/release/publication authorization, if any: actor, source,
  exact action, candidate, target, limits and expiry.
- Next owner and action.

Append decisions; preserve earlier entries. An authenticated record-only decision
may be committed after the frozen candidate without recursively invalidating review
of unchanged candidate content. Any substantive content change reopens affected
review and verification. An instruction to merge/publish is an action authorization,
not retrospective proof that every file received a personal human inspection.
